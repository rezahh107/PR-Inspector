# Owner Output UX Policy

The Persian Owner Decision Card is a decision interface, not a compressed engineering report.

It must state what changed, the practical risk, what was checked, what remains unknown, exactly one next action, and whether specialist review is required.

Hide code, paths, symbols, SHAs, commands, raw logs, evidence labels, capability fields, and rule IDs unless the owner must copy or locate a specific identifier.

Use short plain Persian. Target 120–220 words and never exceed 250 words. Use at most one brief analogy when it materially improves understanding.

Do not claim complete safety, numerical confidence, permanent memory, or automatic handoff.

The owner status and canonical action are derived from technical status and validity by the renderer. Manual divergence invalidates the artifact set.
