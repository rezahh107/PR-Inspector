# Technical Handoff Template

The renderer produces these sections from `review-package.json`:

1. Review Identity
2. Decision Header
3. Capability Manifest
4. Scope and Coverage
5. Change Summary
6. Evidence Records
7. Merge-Blocking Findings
8. Non-Blocking Findings
9. Files Reviewed Outside the Diff
10. Unverified Areas
11. Required Actions Before Merge
12. Out-of-Scope Observations
13. Owner-Card Consistency Map
14. Validation Metadata
15. Final Technical Decision

The rendered handoff is byte-compared with deterministic output during validation.
