# Technical Handoff Template

The renderer produces these sections from `review-package.json`:

1. Review Identity
2. Decision Header
3. Capability Manifest
4. Scope and Coverage
5. Change Summary
6. Intent Fit Evidence
7. Evidence Records
8. Merge-Blocking Findings
9. Non-Blocking Findings
10. Files Reviewed Outside the Diff
11. Unverified Areas
12. Required Actions Before Merge
13. Out-of-Scope Observations
14. Owner-Card Consistency Map
15. Validation Metadata
16. Final Technical Decision

The rendered handoff is byte-compared with deterministic output during validation.
