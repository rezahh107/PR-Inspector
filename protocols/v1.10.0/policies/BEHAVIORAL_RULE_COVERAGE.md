## Enforcement interpretation

- The focused CI command executes the dedicated mutations and the contradictory-action, forged-inspector, missing-artifact, mismatched-hash, identity-binding, replay, orphan-acceptance, and non-acceptable-decision adversarial cases; any violation fails CI.
- `sequence_ci_enforced` is used only for `PRR-PENDING-REREVIEW-001`, because that rule is validated across ordered lifecycle events and verified artifact provenance.
- No row claims `downstream_contract_enforced`; the repository proves producer-side and included-adapter rejection while external consumers remain outside this PR.
- Real cryptographic-signature, production, OS-harness, or independent downstream enforcement is not claimed.
